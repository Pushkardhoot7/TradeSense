📚 Discrete Mathematics — Correlation Theory

What is it?
The Pearson correlation matrix A is an N×N symmetric real matrix where A[i,j] measures the linear relationship between daily returns of stocks i and j.
Graph Construction Rule
We construct G=(V,E) where V = stocks and E = {(i,j) : corr(i,j) ≥ threshold}. No self-loops, no duplicate edges (undirected).
Market Meaning
High correlation → stocks move together → they provide less diversification benefit in a portfolio. Isolated nodes (degree 0) are ideal diversifiers.
