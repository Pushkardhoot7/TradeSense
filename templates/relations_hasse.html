📚 Partial Order Theory

Reflexive
Every stock dominates itself: Return(A)≥Return(A) and Risk(A)≤Risk(A). Always TRUE.
Antisymmetric
If A≽B and B≽A then A=B (or they are tied). Ties form equivalence classes.
Transitive
If A≽B and B≽C then A≽C. Return(A)≥Return(B)≥Return(C) and Risk(A)≤Risk(B)≤Risk(C).
