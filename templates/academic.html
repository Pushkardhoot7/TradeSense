Discrete Mathematics Formalisms & Algorithmic Complexity

TradeSense maps raw continuous financial time-series into discrete algebraic structures: undirected weighted graphs, partially ordered sets (Posets), transitive reductions (DAGs), independent chromatic vertex partitions, and combinatorial powersets.

🕸️

1. Pearson Correlation Graph Construction $G = (V, E)$

Time Complexity: $\mathcal{O}(|V|^2 \cdot T)$

Mathematical Formulation:

$$r_{xy} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^T (R_{x,t} - \bar{R}_x)(R_{y,t} - \bar{R}_y)}{\sqrt{\sum_{t=1}^T (R_{x,t} - \bar{R}_x)^2} \sqrt{\sum_{t=1}^T (R_{y,t} - \bar{R}_y)^2}}$$
$$E = \{ (u, v) \in V \times V \mid u \neq v \text{ and } r_{uv} \ge \theta \}$$

Financial Meaning & Axioms:

  • Symmetry: $r_{xy} = r_{yx}$ ensures an undirected graph.
  • Bounded: $r_{xy} \in [-1, +1]$ allows threshold filtering $\theta \in [0.50, 0.90]$.
  • Graph Sparsification: Eliminates noise and retains only statistically significant co-movement channels.
🎨

2. Welsh-Powell Greedy Vertex Coloring & Independent Sets

Time Complexity: $\mathcal{O}(|V|^2 + |E|)$

Algorithmic Procedure:

  1. Compute vertex degree: $d(v) = |\{u \in V \mid (u,v) \in E\}|$.
  2. Sort vertices in non-increasing order: $d(v_1) \ge d(v_2) \ge \dots \ge d(v_n)$.
  3. Iterate through sorted vertices, assigning the minimal non-conflicting color integer $c \in \{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$.

Portfolio Diversification Theorem:

$$\forall v_i, v_j \in C_k, \quad (v_i, v_j) \notin E \implies r_{v_i, v_j} < \theta$$

Every color class $C_k$ forms an independent set. Selecting assets from distinct color classes strictly bounds cross-asset historical correlation.

3. Partially Ordered Sets (Poset) & Hasse Cover Relation

Transitive Reduction: $\mathcal{O}(|V|^3)$

Dominance Relation $\succeq$:

$$A \succeq B \iff \mu(A) \ge \mu(B) \quad \text{and} \quad \sigma(A) \le \sigma(B)$$
  • Reflexive: $A \succeq A$
  • Antisymmetric: $A \succeq B \land B \succeq A \implies A \sim B$ (modulo tied equivalence)
  • Transitive: $A \succeq B \land B \succeq C \implies A \succeq C$

Transitive Reduction (Hasse Cover DAG):

$$Cover(\succeq) = \{ (A, B) \in \succeq \mid A \neq B \text{ and } \nexists C \text{ s.t. } A \succeq C \succeq B \}$$

Removes redundant transitive shortcuts. Maximal elements (indegree 0 in Cover) represent the Pareto-optimal frontier.

4. Combinatorial Search $C(n, k)$ & 5-Pillar Portfolio Scoring

Combinations: $C(n,k) = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}$

Deterministic Scoring Formula:

$$S_{DM} = 0.30 \cdot S_{ret} + 0.25 \cdot S_{risk} + 0.25 \cdot S_{div} + 0.10 \cdot S_{group} + 0.10 \cdot S_{dom}$$

Component Definitions:

  • $S_{ret}$: Min-max normalized portfolio expected return.
  • $S_{risk}$: Inverted normalized annualized portfolio volatility $\sigma_p = \sqrt{w^T \Sigma w}$.
  • $S_{div}$: Inverted normalized average pairwise correlation.
  • $S_{group}$: Normalized unique chromatic color count.
  • $S_{dom}$: Proportion of constituents belonging to the Pareto-optimal set.
