📚 Graph Coloring Theory

What is Graph Coloring?
Assign colors to vertices such that no two adjacent vertices share a color. The minimum colors needed is the chromatic number χ(G).
Welsh-Powell Algorithm
Greedy coloring by degree order. Sort vertices descending by degree. Assign each the lowest color not used by its already-colored neighbors.
Market Meaning
Stocks in the same color group are "safe" to hold together — they do not have strong correlation under the threshold. Color groups form diversification candidates.
