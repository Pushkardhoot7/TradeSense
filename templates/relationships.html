Understanding Market Connections

How stock relationships impact portfolio risk and diversification

Portfolio Insights
🔗 Co-Moving Connections

A connection line forms when two stocks exhibit strong historical price correlation. Holding multiple connected stocks increases concentration risk if that sector falls.

🛡️ Independent Diversifiers

Stocks with no connecting lines move independently during market swings. Combining unconnected stocks across different groups provides genuine portfolio protection.

📊 Network Density

Network density measures the proportion of possible stock connections that actually exist. Lower density indicates greater opportunities for risk reduction.

⚠️ Portfolio Construction Note

A sparse correlation network indicates lower mutual synchronization among selected stocks under the current threshold. To build a resilient portfolio, select stocks from different clusters or sectors.
