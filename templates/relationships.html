🔗 Connected Components

Maximal subgraphs where every vertex can reach every other vertex via an active path of edges. Stocks within the same component share direct or transitive co-movement.

🛡️ Independent Sets

Sets of vertices containing zero pairwise edges. Stocks in an independent set do not meet the co-movement threshold with one another, providing diversification candidates.

🎨 Graph-Coloring Groups

A partition of the entire graph into disjoint independent sets such that no two adjacent vertices share the same color. Optimal coloring partitions the universe into mutually diversified buckets.

⚠️ Analytical Note on Portfolio Diversification

A sparse correlation network indicates lower pairwise linear synchronization under the chosen threshold, but does not prove complete portfolio risk immunity. Unmodeled macroeconomic shocks or non-linear tail dependence can still affect stocks independently.

Technical & Mathematical Specifications

Discrete Mathematics Specification
1. Edge Qualification Rule

(u, v) ∈ E ⟺ |r_uv| ≥ θ, where r_uv is the Pearson sample correlation between daily log return series of assets u and v over window T.

2. Graph Density Formula

Density D = (2 · |E|) / (|V| · (|V| - 1)). Bounded in [0, 1]. Measures percentage of all C(N, 2) possible market relationships currently realized.

3. Adjacency Representation

G is represented as an adjacency dictionary Adj[u] = {(v, w_uv) : v ∈ N(u)}. Degree d(u) = |Adj[u]| denotes total active connections.

4. Welsh-Powell Chromatic Number

Vertices sorted by descending degree d(v). Greedy sequential assignment gives upper bound χ(G) ≤ Δ(G) + 1 on independent diversification groups.
