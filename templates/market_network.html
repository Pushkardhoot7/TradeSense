💡 What Does This Mean?

Each circle represents a stock. A connecting line means the two stocks have a strong statistical relationship (correlation $r \ge \theta$). More connections indicate that a stock moves tightly in tandem with the general market. Fewer connections highlight independent, diversifying assets.

🎯 Why It Matters For Investors

Combining stocks that share multiple connecting lines creates concentrated market risk, as they tend to decline together in downturns. Selecting assets with no direct connections provides genuine diversification, smoothing volatility and enhancing risk efficiency.
