💡 What Are Stock Groups?

Our graph-coloring engine groups stocks such that assets sharing a strong co-movement relationship ($r \ge \theta$) are assigned to different groups. Stocks in the same group have low mutual co-movement, making them natural diversification partners.

🎯 Why It Matters For Portfolio Construction

Selecting one stock from each separate group creates an Independent Set where no two assets have high correlation. This provides mathematical protection against sector contagion and reduces portfolio volatility during broad market pullbacks.
